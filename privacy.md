# E.N.G.R.A.M. — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 13 October 2026  
**Last Updated:** 13 October 2026

## 1. Introduction

E.N.G.R.A.M. (Executive Neural Guidance for Research, Automation & Management) is an independently developed, locally hosted AI assistant and creator analytics platform operated under the name **E.N.G.R.A.M. Analytics**.

This Privacy Policy explains how E.N.G.R.A.M. accesses, processes, stores, protects, and deletes information when users connect supported services, including TikTok and YouTube.

E.N.G.R.A.M. is designed around a local-first architecture. However, authorised API integrations require communication with external platforms.

## 2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features enabled and permissions granted, E.N.G.R.A.M. may process:

- **Account information:** Usernames, account identifiers, profile names, and profile images.
- **Creator statistics:** Follower counts, subscriber counts, video counts, views, likes, and other available engagement metrics.
- **Video information:** Video identifiers, titles, descriptions, publication dates, and available performance statistics.
- **Authentication information:** OAuth access tokens, refresh tokens, and developer integration credentials.
- **Application information:** User preferences, connected-account settings, and relevant application activity.

Additional information may be processed when users explicitly enable other E.N.G.R.A.M. features.

## 3. TikTok Data Access

E.N.G.R.A.M. uses TikTok's official APIs to access information authorised by the account holder.

Depending on TikTok's approved permissions, this may include:

- Basic TikTok profile information.
- Profile statistics, including follower and like counts.
- Authorised video lists.
- Available video engagement metrics.

E.N.G.R.A.M. accesses only information permitted by the scopes granted during TikTok authorisation.

TikTok account information is used to populate the Analytics Hub and provide creator performance insights.

E.N.G.R.A.M. does not use TikTok credentials to access accounts without authorisation or sell TikTok account data.

## 4. YouTube and Google Data Access

E.N.G.R.A.M. uses Google OAuth and official YouTube APIs to retrieve authorised account information.

This may include:

- YouTube channel names and identifiers.
- Subscriber counts.
- Channel view totals.
- Published video counts.
- Recent video information.
- Available video engagement statistics.

Information retrieved through Google APIs is used to display channel statistics and support creator analytics features requested by the user.

E.N.G.R.A.M.'s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including applicable Limited Use requirements.

Google and YouTube data is not sold to advertisers or data brokers.

## 5. How Information Is Used

Information obtained through supported integrations is used to:

1. Authenticate connected accounts with user consent.
2. Retrieve permitted creator and video statistics.
3. Display account and content performance in the Analytics Hub.
4. Refresh authorised information when requested.
5. Support user-requested analysis and reporting.
6. Maintain connection status and identify integration errors.

E.N.G.R.A.M. does not automatically publish TikTok or YouTube content through its current read-only Analytics Hub integrations.

## 6. Data Storage and Security

E.N.G.R.A.M. is designed to operate on a user-controlled computer or server.

Within its current creator analytics implementation:

- OAuth credentials are stored locally using the application's encrypted credential-storage mechanism.
- Account information and retrieved analytics may be processed or cached locally.
- Account access is controlled through the application's existing authentication and authorisation mechanisms.
- API communication uses the supported HTTPS endpoints of the relevant providers.

No security mechanism can guarantee absolute protection against unauthorised access.

Users are responsible for maintaining appropriate security on devices hosting E.N.G.R.A.M.

## 7. Data Sharing and External Services

E.N.G.R.A.M. communicates with TikTok, Google, and YouTube when users initiate or enable their respective integrations.

Requests to those services may transmit OAuth credentials and technical information necessary to authenticate requests and retrieve authorised data.

Other locally configured AI or third-party integrations may have separate data-handling requirements.

E.N.G.R.A.M. does not sell personal information or share creator analytics with advertising networks as part of its current Analytics Hub functionality.

## 8. Data Retention

E.N.G.R.A.M. retains locally stored account credentials and related integration data as necessary to maintain authorised functionality, subject to the application's actual configuration.

Disconnecting a connected account removes its locally stored OAuth connection credentials through the supported disconnection functionality.

Previously generated reports, downloaded content, logs, backups, or other application records may require separate removal.

Cached API readings are generally temporary and may be refreshed or replaced.

Retention periods for other E.N.G.R.A.M. features may vary.

## 9. Account Disconnection and Access Revocation

Users may disconnect supported accounts through E.N.G.R.A.M.'s Analytics Hub.

Users may also revoke permissions directly through their platform account settings.

For Google, permissions can be reviewed at:

https://myaccount.google.com/connections

For TikTok, users can review connected applications through TikTok's account security and application-authorisation settings.

Revoking access prevents the application from continuing to use the revoked authorisation, subject to provider behaviour and any other independently authorised connections.

## 10. Data Deletion Requests

Users may request deletion of personal information held by the E.N.G.R.A.M. operator, where applicable.

Requests may be submitted through the project's official GitHub repository:

https://github.com/engram-analytics/engram-analytics.github.io

Users operating their own local E.N.G.R.A.M. installation can also manage locally stored application data, subject to the application's available deletion controls.

A deletion request should identify the connected platform and the information to be removed without including passwords, OAuth tokens, or other sensitive credentials.

Requests will be handled in accordance with applicable legal obligations and technical capabilities.

## 11. User Control and Consent

Connecting TikTok or YouTube requires the user's explicit authorisation through the relevant platform's OAuth consent process.

Users may choose not to connect accounts.

The Analytics Hub displays disconnected or unavailable states where the required permissions or credentials are absent.

Users can revoke supported integrations when they no longer wish to share authorised account information.

## 12. Children's Privacy

E.N.G.R.A.M. is not intended to circumvent TikTok, YouTube, Google, or other platforms' minimum-age requirements.

Users must satisfy the applicable eligibility requirements for each connected account.

The application is not designed to knowingly collect children's personal information without a lawful basis or required consent.

## 13. International Data Processing

E.N.G.R.A.M. is operated from Australia and is designed for local processing.

Connected services such as TikTok, Google, and YouTube may process information using infrastructure located in other countries under their respective policies.

Users should review the privacy terms of connected platforms for further information about those services' processing practices.

## 14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when E.N.G.R.A.M. adds integrations, changes its data-handling practices, or must comply with new legal or platform requirements.

Changes will be reflected in the Last Updated date at the top of this document.
